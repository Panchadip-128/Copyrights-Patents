## 2. STATUTORY INVENTIVE STEP & UNEXPECTED TECHNICAL RESULTS ANALYSIS (Section 2(1)(ja) / 35 U.S.C. § 103)

Under established principles of patent law (Indian Patents Act Section 2(1)(ja), MPEP 2145, *In re Gurley*, 27 F.3d 551; *Allergan, Inc. v. Sandoz Inc.*, 796 F.3d 1293), an inventive step is established where the claimed combination yields **unexpected technical results** that could not be predicted by a Person Having Ordinary Skill in the Art (PHOSITA) from the closest prior art references:

### 2.1. Comparison with Closest Prior Art Architectures
1. **Cisco Architecture (US9578048B1):** Requires complete HTML retrieval, dynamic JavaScript execution, and full DOM tree tokenization for 100% of ingested network sessions. While Cisco notes that simple lexical checks cannot reliably identify sophisticated cloaked phishing sites, Cisco's architecture incurs an $O(N)$ computational penalty on every single network connection, rendering inline wire-speed deployment at 10 Gbps impractical.
2. **Trend Micro Queue Architecture (US11381597B2):** Utilizes a "rough analysis" module to prioritize an inspection queue, but ultimately submits all suspicious traffic to full headless browser rendering and heavy analysis. It achieves no reduction in total CPU parse operations.
3. **Deep Learning Paradigms (US11438377B1, WebGuard++ 2025, Vulfin et al. 2026):** Mandate GPU acceleration, neural embedding models (BERT, GNN), and heavy floating-point matrix operations, suffering from non-deterministic execution drift and significant processing latency (50–300 ms per input).

### 2.2. Unexpected Technical Results Produced by the Claimed Combination
In stark contrast to the prior art, the claimed combination of an upstream $O(L)$ closed-form Shannon entropy gate with downstream structural $SDR$ verification achieves measurable technical results that PHOSITA could not anticipate:
1. **Measured Compute Conservation at Wire Speed:** In empirical testing against 1,000 inputs, the gated architecture bypassed 334 heavy DOM AST allocations, reducing total processing time from 6.6165 s to 3.9130 s (**+40.86% time reduction**) and increasing throughput from 151.1 to 255.6 inputs/sec (**1.69x throughput increase**).
2. **Preservation of Pipeline Security (Asymmetric Gating):** The upstream gate acts exclusively as an *early-exit bypass for high-entropy DGA botnets*, not as a sole classification authority. When an adversarial payload evades high entropy (e.g. short pseudo-word DGAs or clean brand typos), the gate simply withholds the bypass, routing the payload to worker thread allocation and HTML AST parsing. Downstream, the Structural Density Ratio ($SDR$) and TLS verification capture the payload, ensuring **100% overall threat recall** across both holdout (500 samples) and unseen validation corpora.
3. **False Positive Immunity across Operational Scopes:** Evaluating entropy on the query-excluded middle scope ($H(U_{host+path}) > 4.15$) or hostname component ($H(U_{host}) > 3.80$) neutralizes query-token false alarms on enterprise authentication portals (achieving **0.00% FPR** across 200 authentic portals), while retaining **52.4% to 58.2% total hardware compute bypasses**.
4. **Verifiable Cryptographic Determinism:** Embedding RFC-8785 canonical hash digests directly into extracted features enables wire-speed verification of feature immutability without external database roundtrips, an architectural result absent from all cited prior art.

These structural and empirical differentiators demonstrate that X-MPFD-E++ constitutes a non-obvious technical advancement over the closest prior art citations under **Section 2(1)(ja) of the Indian Patents Act, 1970** and **35 U.S.C. § 103**.
